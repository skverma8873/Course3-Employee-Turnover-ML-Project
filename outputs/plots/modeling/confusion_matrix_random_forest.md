# Analysis: Confusion Matrix — Random Forest

## What is a Confusion Matrix?
A confusion matrix compares what the model predicted against what actually happened, for every employee in the test set (3,000 employees).

| | Model predicted: Stay | Model predicted: Leave |
|---|---|---|
| **Actually Stayed** | True Negative (TN) ✓ | False Positive (FP) ✗ |
| **Actually Left** | False Negative (FN) ✗ | True Positive (TP) ✓ |

- **TN:** Correctly identified a stayer — no wasted effort.
- **TP:** Correctly identified a leaver — HR can intervene in time.
- **FP:** Flagged as leaver but stayed — unnecessary intervention.
- **FN:** Flagged as stayer but actually left — **the costly miss**.

---

## Results for Random Forest

**Test set: 3,000 employees (2,286 stayed, 714 left)**

| | Predicted Stay | Predicted Leave |
|---|---|---|
| **Actually Stayed** | TN = **2,272** | FP = **14** |
| **Actually Left** | FN = **15** | TP = **699** |

---

## Interpreting the Numbers

**Missed Departures (FN = 15):**
Only 15 out of 714 real leavers were missed. This is exceptional performance. Out of every 100 employees who actually resigned, the model correctly flagged 98 of them in advance.

**False Alarms (FP = 14):**
Only 14 employees who stayed were incorrectly flagged. HR can act on almost every alert from this model without wasting resources on unlikely risks.

**Total Wrong = 29 out of 3,000 = 99.0% overall accuracy.**

---

## Derived Metrics

| Metric | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Recall (Left)** | 699 / (699+15) = **97.9%** | Catches 98 in every 100 real leavers |
| **Precision (Left)** | 699 / (699+14) = **98.0%** | 98% of flagged employees are genuine risks |
| **Overall Accuracy** | (2272+699) / 3000 = **99.0%** | Correct on 99% of all predictions |

## HR Decision-Making Implications

- **This is the selected production model.** Every risk score in the retention report is generated by Random Forest.
- With only 15 missed leavers out of 714, HR can be confident that the vast majority of genuine flight risks will surface in the retention report before they resign.
- With only 14 false alarms, HR conversations triggered by this model's alerts are almost always warranted — no need to worry about over-intervening with employees who plan to stay.
- The model's near-perfect performance means HR can focus energy on **response quality** (what to offer, how to engage) rather than **filtering noise** (which alerts are real).
